Over the weekend, my team placed 1st at the Brampton SecOps Hackathon! This was an intense competition where top talent in technology and cybersecurity came together to tackle critical cybersecurity challenges facing small-to-medium businesses. With help and feedback from the mentors, we were able to zero in on the winning idea and perfect our pitch.

It was also a really special moment to have our team recognized by the Office of MP Amandeep Sodhi.
![Tina Ismail Certificate](/assets/img/BramptonSecOps/TinaIsmail.png)

We are incredibly proud of this win and excited to see where this journey takes us next!

A huge thank you to my amazing teammates, Sulaksa and Jasraj, for your hard work and effort this weekend.
